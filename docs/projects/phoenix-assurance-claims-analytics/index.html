01 / THE BUSINESS CHALLENGE

Start with the right question.

Claims teams need visibility into both financial progress and processing stages. Approved, paid, rejected and unpaid claims must be read alongside service levels and hospital turnaround time.

02 / DATA SOURCES & OBJECTIVES

A useful foundation.

Based on Pheonix Insurance Pvt Ltd .pbix. Its embedded logo identifies Phoenix Assurance Private Limited. The public demonstration uses fictional aggregate case and claim figures without patient or policyholder records.

  • Summarize cases, approved claims, paid claims and unpaid amounts.
  • Review claim processing service levels and workflow bottlenecks.
  • Compare claims across hospitals, districts, specialties and demographic groups.
03 / ANALYTICAL APPROACH

From raw information to a clear view.

Separate the claims overview from processing detail. Use case status, payment progress, service levels and workflow roles to make the path from initiation to payment easier to review.

Data preparation

The source report includes admission dates, hospital and district attributes, case types and statuses, workflow roles and payment flags. Aggregate comparisons use consistent claim definitions.

Technical implementation

Power BI measures cover cases, approvals, payments, rejected and unpaid claims, families covered, surgeries, service-level compliance, discharge turnaround time and tax deductions.

04 / QUESTIONS TO EXPLORE

What the dashboard helps you investigate.

  • Where do claims accumulate in the processing workflow?
  • How do approved and paid claim amounts compare?
  • Which hospitals or districts need a closer service-level review?

Verified findings are not published for this demonstration. The preview illustrates the reporting experience; conclusions would require validation against the actual source data and business context.
