01 / THE BUSINESS CHALLENGE

Start with the right question.

A food and beverage inventory review needs to connect current stock, incoming supply and sales velocity. Stock shortages and products approaching expiry need to be visible alongside the broader picture.

02 / DATA SOURCES & OBJECTIVES

A useful foundation.

Based on the structure of Supply_Chain.pbix. The public showcase uses newly generated figures, fictional product names and generalized regions throughout. Its layout and formatting have been redesigned; original operational values and source report images are not published.

  • Compare stock on hand, customer orders and incoming quantities across regions.
  • Review sales run rate and stock cover to guide replenishment.
  • Surface expiry exposure, write-off risk and out-of-stock exceptions.
03 / ANALYTICAL APPROACH

From raw information to a clear view.

Organize the inventory review around availability, demand and risk. Start with a summary, then use regional filters, stock-versus-demand comparisons and a replenishment priority list to direct attention.

Data preparation

The source report separates item master, state, date, stock history, shipment and velocity information. The demonstration uses a fictional dataset with consistent units, product keys and reporting periods.

Technical implementation

Power BI report measures cover stock on hand, incoming quantity, customer orders, sales run rate, weeks on hand and expiry-related exposure. The website recreates those analytical questions in a new interactive layout with synthetic values.

04 / QUESTIONS TO EXPLORE

What the dashboard helps you investigate.

  • Where does stock cover fall short of demand?
  • Which products need replenishment attention?
  • Where is expiry exposure concentrated?

Verified findings are not published for this demonstration. The preview illustrates the reporting experience; conclusions would require validation against the actual source data and business context.
