A FEW COMMON QUESTIONS

Before we say hello.

01What types of businesses do you work with?

I work with small and medium-sized businesses, startups, e-commerce businesses, and finance, operations, sales, and supply chain teams. The starting point is your reporting challenge and the data you already have.

02Can you automate my existing Excel reports?

Yes. I can review your existing files and suggest a practical workflow using Power Query, advanced formulas, VBA, or Python. The level of automation depends on your data sources, access, and available infrastructure.

03Can you build dashboards using my existing data?

Yes. Excel files, databases, and other structured sources can form the basis of a dashboard. I first assess data quality, access requirements, and the questions the dashboard needs to answer.

04Do you work with international clients?

I am open to working with businesses in India and internationally. Scope, communication, time zone overlap, currency, and delivery arrangements are agreed before a project starts.
