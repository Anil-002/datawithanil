BEFORE WE GET STARTED

A little more clarity.

Ask me something else
01What types of businesses do you work with?

I work with small and medium-sized businesses, startups, e-commerce businesses, and finance, operations, sales, and supply chain teams. The starting point is your reporting challenge and the data you already have.

02Can you automate my existing Excel reports?

Yes. I can review your existing files and suggest a practical workflow using Power Query, advanced formulas, VBA, or Python. The level of automation depends on your data sources, access, and available infrastructure.

03Can you build dashboards using my existing data?

Yes. Excel files, databases, and other structured sources can form the basis of a dashboard. I first assess data quality, access requirements, and the questions the dashboard needs to answer.

04Do you work with international clients?

I am open to working with businesses in India and internationally. Scope, communication, time zone overlap, currency, and delivery arrangements are agreed before a project starts.

05Can you work with multiple Excel files?

Yes. I can consolidate multiple files, standardize their formats, and create a repeatable preparation process. I will need a representative sample and an understanding of how new files arrive.

06Do you provide ongoing dashboard maintenance?

Ongoing maintenance and support can be scoped separately. This may include data-source updates, measure changes, new report pages, and troubleshooting, according to an agreed arrangement.

07How long does a dashboard project take?

Timing depends on the number of data sources, data quality, reporting requirements, and review cycles. After assessing your requirements, I will propose a realistic schedule rather than promise a fixed turnaround in advance.

08What do you need to start a project?

A description of your business problem, the reports you use today, the decisions you want to support, and a representative data sample are helpful. Please avoid sending passwords or confidential datasets through the inquiry form.
