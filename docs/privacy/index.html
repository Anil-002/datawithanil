Privacy Policy

Clear information about using this website and getting in touch.

Who handles your information

Data With Anil is the independent consulting brand of Anil Singh. This notice explains how information submitted through this website is used.

Information you choose to provide

The contact page links to your email app. The website does not receive or store a project inquiry when you open this link. If you send an email, Anil receives the information you choose to include through the email provider.

Please do not include passwords, financial account information, personal customer records, or confidential datasets in the form.

How your information is used

Your details are used to understand your request, respond to you, discuss a possible engagement, and maintain relevant project correspondence. They are not submitted to an advertising mailing list by this form.

Storage and service providers

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Email inquiries are handled through the email provider; there is no inquiry database or submission form on this version of the site.

Hosting logs and anti-spam controls may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and request timing, to keep the form operational and prevent abuse.

Retention and your choices

Inquiry details should be kept only as long as needed for the inquiry, any resulting engagement, and applicable recordkeeping obligations. You may request access, correction, or deletion by emailing Anil using the address below. Some records may need to be retained where required for legal or contractual reasons.

Cookies and analytics

The default website does not enable advertising trackers or non-essential analytics. If optional Google Analytics is configured, it loads only after you choose to allow it. Page-visit measurements are sent without URL query strings or inquiry field values. Your choice is stored in this browser. Use Privacy settings to change your choice; withdrawing consent disables future tracking and clears this site’s Google Analytics cookies.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated when the website’s services or data handling changes. Review it before sending information if you have questions about how your inquiry will be used.
